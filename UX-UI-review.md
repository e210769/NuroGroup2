# NURA Group — seven UX/UI improvements

Review based on the current redesign on desktop and 390px mobile layouts in Uzbek, Russian and English. Golos Text has been implemented. The following are recommendations, not additional implemented changes.

1. **State the business immediately.** The opening headline, Building tomorrow. Together., communicates ambition but leaves visitors to interpret the business. Lead with energy, engineering, manufacturing and distribution, then use the brand statement as supporting copy. This directly supports your goal of helping visitors understand NURA.

2. **Keep contact options within reach.** The phone number disappears from the header below 1000px, and Contact is absent from the desktop navigation. Add a clear Contact action on desktop and an accessible call action on mobile. Visitors should be able to contact the group from a company, news or project page without finding the footer.

3. **Explain the company structure more clearly.** Give every company a short description of its role within the three divisions. Explicitly label NURA Electric's engineering and manufacturing roles; it currently appears twice while the site reports nine business units. This will help visitors understand the relationship between the companies.

4. **Shorten the first screen on mobile.** The heading, buttons, large photographs and experience badge occupy more than a phone screen before the group structure begins. Reduce the photo height and decorative spacing, and introduce the three divisions sooner. Keep one prominent experience metric instead of repeating 19 years in adjacent sections.

5. **Improve small text and controls.** Some labels are around 10–13px and use pale grey. Increase regularly used labels to about 14px and body copy to at least 16px, darken secondary text, and enlarge the language and menu tap areas. Retain the existing focus styles and reduced-motion support.

6. **Make project cards prove capability.** Titles such as BOSTON 110/35/10 kV are meaningful to engineers but provide limited context for other visitors. Add the location, NURA's role and one verified result. Structure detailed case studies around the challenge, work delivered and outcome, with technical specifications below.

7. **Complete enquiries within the new site.** The enquiry, partnership and application buttons currently send visitors to the existing website. Use focused forms on the new site, route submissions to the appropriate team, and show a clear success or error state. This will also make the eventual domain migration easier.
